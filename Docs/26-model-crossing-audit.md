# Crossing checks for existing models

`npm run audit:model-crossings` inspects every mapped single-path trace, including layouts that already have borders and a model. It records exact source snapshot and trace hashes. Composed networks delegate to their registered component layouts, which are checked independently. Networks without a composition remain unverified; run `validate:networks` to check composition hashes and complete component round-tripping.

The audit distinguishes a source bridge candidate, an explicit source-backed profile, a missing profile and an unexplained intersection. A bridge tag alone does not establish its height. Existing profiles must pass source-version and ribbon-crossing verification. A model's existence does not prove that its road levels are correct.

For playable models with a source crossing, the audit rebuilds the road and locates the corresponding runtime intersection. It interpolates the two saved road heights and widths at that intersection. Multiple nearby intersections, missing matches and reconstruction errors remain unresolved. Values describe the current reconstruction, not physical measurements or vehicle clearance. The three-metre difference is a diagnostic threshold, not permission to invent a bridge height. Unexplained intersections can include at-grade junctions and retraces; they require identity and imagery review rather than an automatic bridge classification.

The report is `sources/research/model-crossing-audit.json`. It includes four playable layouts with source bridge candidates but no explicit profile. Two Suzuka layouts, Central Circuit, Spa Nishiura and Parcmotor Castellolí have source-backed profiles. The 123 topology events in Castle Combe's held Western sprint are largely retraces, not 123 bridges. Counts describe events separately from layouts.

`npm run validate:model-crossings` checks that the report matches the current files. It is part of the build. A fresh report is not a declaration that its unresolved findings are fixed. Complete coverage requires reviewing those findings as well as missing borders, unavailable routes and unmapped entries. The audit changes no geometry, borders, timing, model recipes or UI availability.

Highlands provides a concrete example. Its existing full-course model reconstructs the crossing with only about 0.1 m between road levels and a brief width of about 31 m. The 2023–2024 LINZ image clearly shows the bridge. The elevation collections checked so far do not provide a covering tile at that location; a regional dataset title is insufficient evidence. The source proof, crop, attribution and checked URLs are in `sources/research/highlands-bridge-evidence.json`. An actual covering elevation source and correspondence-preserving road sections are needed before accepting corrected bridge playback.

Central Circuit now has paired image-estimated limits and separate upper/lower approach profiles in its existing GeoJSON. The source bridge way and version remain pinned. Its reconstructed levels differ by about 4.8 m at the crossing, with no repaired road sections. The original unequal border paths and model are retained in [correction evidence](../sources/research/central-circuit-bridge-correction.json). Image resolution and visual edge reading do not establish surveyed accuracy. The remaining four playable bridge candidates still need their own evidence and correction.

Spa Nishiura now reconstructs its crossing with about 5.1 m between the estimated road levels. Its source trace starts on a bridge approach, so the paired grid shifts by 259 of 519 stations. The original trace and timing gate stay exact; both approach intervals remain continuous inside the height profile. Independent image controls and separate GSI approach samples replace the flattened crossing without a nominal height. Original borders and the old model remain in [correction evidence](../sources/research/nishiura-bridge-correction.json).

Parcmotor Castellolí now reconstructs separate road levels with about 15.6 m between them at the crossing and no repaired sections. Its single layout GeoJSON contains 630 compact paired-edge controls, the original OSM bridge identity and 46 height anchors derived from the ICGC 2021–2023 native 50 cm terrain raster. The cropped Float32 ground cells agree with 32 independent numeric WMS queries. Both roads interpolate between their own ground approaches; the estimate is not surveyed clearance. The original trace, timing, borders and old model remain available in the [correction evidence](../sources/research/parcmotor-bridge-correction.json).

# Palanga street-course review

The [19 July 2014 Promaksa / EPIC Media onboard](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VF2tTIVQ5Rg) identifies the post-reconstruction road sequence: northwest clover loop, southbound A13, paved southern median-end turn beside the service station, northbound A13, outer northeast approach and westbound A11. The [organizer's 2014 results](https://racing.lt/media/rezultatai/2014/Final.pdf) identify a 2,682 m event course. It is distinct from the earlier 2,994 m configuration. The [municipality's 2014 traffic notice](https://www.palanga.lt/naujienos/eismo-organizavimas-palangoje-1000-km-varzybu-metu/2672?lang=en) establishes that the race closes the public roads. Video and operator diagrams are identity references only; they are not traced, georeferenced or redistributed.

The supporting physical-course draft uses 18 exact public OSM ways. Every segment and join retains source node identities, indices and versions. The northeast approach is traversed opposite to civilian traffic; its original one-way tags remain unchanged. No artificial link closes the route. The earlier local candidate's southeast civilian ramp and eastern A11 turnaround are excluded. The independently licensed May 2023 ORT10LT imagery corroborates the paved median turn and road corridors but supplies no recipe coordinates.

The physical road centerlines measure 2,727.1 m. The draft retains a 65.1 m discrepancy from the supplied 2,662 m scalar and 45.1 m from the organizer's 2,682 m description. Temporary chicanes, barrier turns and event lane positions are not independently mapped. The 100 m source allowance preserves the discrepancy without adding turns, moving nodes or fitting the route to either length. This is supporting physical-course geometry, not a certified race-day lap. Public timing GPS is absent; the supplied marker stays in the local preview overlay.

The selection manifest preserves the exact source-way sequence. The parent road snapshot, its acquisition query and hash are pinned in `sources/course-networks/palanga-street-current-independent-roads/`. The selected snapshot and its fingerprint are also preserved here. Regeneration verifies the pinned versions and shared-node joins. OSM attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors; ODbL 1.0.

The [imagery coverage review](../research/palanga-orthophoto-coverage/review.md) preserves the separate licensed pavement evidence. Its earlier claim that the southern event turn must be farther south is withdrawn: a course-length mismatch alone did not establish that claim. The newly reviewed primary onboard identifies the paved median-end turn. Precise temporary geometry remains unresolved.

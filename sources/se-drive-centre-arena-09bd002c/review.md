# Drive Centre Arena course review

The long airfield course has a supporting draft digitized from native 0.5 m Lantmäteriet Min karta aerial imagery. The [operator track-day page](https://www.drivecenter.se/trackdays/) identifies a 4,235 m long layout and 3,370 m training layout. Its linked course graphic and the [official STCC 2020 course guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0_dedeDGcjg) establish the branch order: inner eastern S bends, northern diagonal runway, outer eastern return, southern S connector, runway turnaround and western bulb. The short training connection and pit lane are excluded. Operator diagrams and video are identity evidence only; they supply no coordinates and are not redistributed.

The fine licensed aerial image resolves the painted runway turnaround and the paved curves. Coordinates are selected only from that raster. The earlier coarse-image candidate and erroneous local vertices outside the eastern return pavement are excluded. Public road ways corroborate the pavement arrangement but supply no recipe coordinates. The identified 2013 public snowmobile recording predates the racecourse and remains excluded.

The independently drawn physical course measures 4,415.4 m. Its 146.4 m discrepancy from the supplied 4,269 m scalar and 180.4 m discrepancy from the operator's 4,235 m description remain explicit. The imagery capture year is not identified by the public viewer. The source resolves pavement, but historical event correspondence, temporary cone placement and exact corner alignment remain draft. The 200 m source-specific distance allowance records these discrepancies; no geometry is fitted to either scalar and no timing threshold is relaxed. This supporting trace is not a certified event lap. Public timing GPS is absent; the supplied marker stays in the private preview overlay.

`min-karta-import-drive-centre.json` preserves 125 exact public WMS tile URLs, hashes, dimensions and EPSG:3006 bounds, the native mosaic, its affine transform, native digitization vertices and the EPSG:3857 rendering. Black areas contain no acquired tiles; no route vertex lies in those areas. Intermediate vertices on long straight segments add no detail. Native pixel centers are converted through the recorded projections without snapping to timing data.

[Provider reuse terms](https://www.lantmateriet.se/minkarta) permit images and screenshots under CC BY 4.0 with attribution and notice of modification: Datakälla Lantmäteriets Min karta; ©Lantmäteriet; bearbetad av OpenRacetrackDB; CC BY 4.0. The source snapshots retain this attribution.

Run `python3 sources/se-drive-centre-arena-09bd002c/reproduce-raster.py` with numpy, Pillow and rasterio to verify pinned hashes, georeferencing and every rendered pixel. Add `--online` to verify all 125 public tile hashes through two ordinary anonymous map views. Direct WMS export requests return empty bodies, so verification uses the same public viewer acquisition as the original capture; no credentials or request overrides are used. The script reads no private archive.

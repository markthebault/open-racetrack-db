# Historical Bikernieki rallycross configuration

The selected configuration retains the northwestern S, western long hairpin, eastern inward step, short southeastern triangle and parallel finish straight. The FIA standard and joker plans are context references for distinct event laps, not proof of equivalence with this historical configuration.

Exact 2017 public road nodes supply the mapped branches. The licensed LGIA cycle-six aerial supplies the missing upper step onto the straight and confirms that both it and the short southeastern connector lie on pavement. Public node coordinates are projected unchanged into the pinned raster grid. Their provenance and the independently selected connector pixels are recorded in the import metadata. Both OSM and LGIA attributions remain in the download.

This is a draft. The catalogue distance is retained without scaling the route. Tile flight date, event barrier placement and historical-year correspondence remain unresolved. The later long southeastern extension and alternate current finish approach are excluded. No private course coordinates or diagram pixels supply any part of the trace.

# MC Motorsport Main Track

The January 5, 2026 Copernicus Sentinel-2 raster is pinned with its native 10 m survey metadata, Web Mercator export extent, image hash and manually reviewed pixel vertices. Independent public GPS mapping samples corroborate the northern return and narrower western main-course loop. An inspection record preserves the public response hash, sample counts and source URL, with no GPS coordinates. The anonymous legacy samples have date-dependent redistribution terms, so they are used only to identify course branches. No trip order, timestamp or timing is inferred. Geometry is separately digitized from the licensed georeferenced satellite raster; no sample coordinates are imported or redistributed.

The earlier trial outlines selected the northern training pad or larger western skidpan, and remain excluded. The [2016 organizer handbook](https://mgcc.com.au/natmeets/2016-natmeet-program.pdf) confirms the broader venue's additional skidpan branches, used by its longer sprint. The [operator training document](https://www.driverrisk.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/14.-DRM-Level-1-Performance-Driver-Training-Course.pdf) identifies the main training facility. These documents and the supplied layout facts identify the course only; no diagram is traced or georeferenced.

The draft retains the main northern teardrop, central western bend and inner return, long parallel southwestern legs and southern U-turn. The larger western skidpan, separate northern pad, access roads and southern parking loops are excluded. Independent raster geometry measures 1,524.9 m; its 24.9 m supplied-distance difference is retained, without distance scaling. Native imagery resolution, GPS sampling uncertainty, fine corner radii and historical alignment remain draft. Closed geometry has no crossing or invented endpoint connection. Public timing is absent; supplied GPS remains only in the local preview overlay.

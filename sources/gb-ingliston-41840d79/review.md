# Ingliston South Track

The operator identifies the southern original circuit connected by a newer closing section: https://www.ingliston.co.uk/two-supercars-drift-experience/ . The listed simple boomerang configuration uses the tiny western hairpin and northeast return, excluding the Grandstand arena, eastern service-road grid and optional later chicane.

All published course coordinates are exact independently mapped OSM nodes. The five selected ways close through shared node IDs. Their complete selection measures 1,062.7 m; the listed scalar is 1,061 m. No coordinate is adjusted to match a distance or diagram. Source versions, segment indices, snapshot hash and timing compatibility are pinned by the course-selection recipe.

The cropped one-metre intensity raster independently verifies the paved route. It is derived from first returns in Scottish Government Phase 4 LAS tiles NT1372, NT1373, NT1472 and NT1473 (2017–2019). Dataset-specific metadata confirms Open Government Licence v3, EPSG:27700 and four points per square metre. Mean native one-metre intensity, global percentile contrast stretching and nearest-neighbour reprojection retain the independent raster registration. Acquisition URLs, input hashes, bounds and processing are retained in `scottish-phase4-acquisition.json`. No supplied timing or course coordinates enter raster processing.

## Racing-course source correction

The original selection used the named Calle Boxes pit road and the alternative loop between Curvas8 and9. The WorldSBK2021 track map explicitly distinguishes pit and long-lap penalty roads from the racing course. Native imagery confirms these separate paved branches. The corrected selection uses only exact connected, forward OSM node sequences from the unchanged snapshot, including the main straight and full Curvas1 through17. No diagram coordinates, GPS table values or nominal distance are fitted. Original timing stays estimated and unchanged. The previous recipe and trace are retained in the shared road-limit measurement review. Road limits require a separate image review.

# Ruapuna

The four Grand Prix and outer configurations follow exact independent OSM source-node routes. Their coordinates, borders, timing and models remain unchanged.

The short Eastern route is independently digitized from March 2025 Environment Canterbury imagery via LINZ, CC BY 4.0. It replaces an OSM junction that doubled back 44 m along the main straight. The image, its georeferencing and original source recipes are retained for reproduction. Published circuit plans identify branches only; no diagram coordinates are copied. Historical alignment and direction remain draft.

The combined entry rebuilds these five components independently. Each source snapshot and exact selected trace is hash pinned. No coordinates are snapped and no connector is invented. Only exactly shared coordinate edges count once in the displayed branch length. Nearby overlapping image and OSM paths remain coordinate-distinct, so that length is not a unique pavement distance or a single lap.

# Adria regional coverage review

The publicly accessible regional WFS returns 28 roads around the venue under IODL 2.0. The pinned road-network response and acquisition record reproduce this coverage inspection. Those roads do not include the racetrack pavement, so none are registered as layout geometry. Attribution: Regione del Veneto; [dataset and reuse terms](https://idt2.regione.veneto.it/geoportal/catalog/search/resource/details.page?uuid=r_veneto%3Ac01070240012_ElementoStradale).

The geotopographic survey covering this area is LOTTO BASSO POLESINE, with aerial survey year 2008. The year-review file preserves the public request, response fingerprint and metadata facts. It does not copy the survey-area boundary. This source predates the 2021 inner extension.

Clear Copernicus Sentinel-2 imagery from 2021-11-24 independently shows the new pavement, but its native ten-metre pixels do not establish every tight switchback. The earlier partial-swath candidates had missing pixels over the course and were rejected. No trial trace is registered from these images. The 2021 course remains an unresolved layout.

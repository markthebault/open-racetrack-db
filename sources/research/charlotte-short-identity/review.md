# Charlotte historical Short identity review

The frontstretch pavement is visible in independently georeferenced public 2014 and 2022 NAIP imagery. Course identity remains unresolved because that pavement supports several configurations, while the supplied entry is separate from the full oval, infield and later ROVAL entries.

[Second Strike's 2004 organizer page](https://www.secondstrike.com/Events/Spring2004/Main.html) identifies a 0.7-mile **infield** driver-education course, with the banked oval used separately. Page 13 of the organizer's [2000 newsletter](https://www.secondstrike.com/Newsletter/SecondStrike_V3N3.pdf) shows the eight-turn infield plan. That first-hand historical evidence identifies a different part of the venue and is excluded as an explanation of the requested frontstretch branches. Neither plan is traced, georeferenced or redistributed.

[Makita's 2012 event announcement](https://makitaassemblytools.com/company/press-releases/2012/makita-and-brian-deegan-set-for-2012-global-rallycross-championship) and [Scott-Eklund Racing's team announcement](https://news.cision.com/hazel-pr/r/media-release--scott-eklund-racing-set-for-grc-charlotte-motor-speedway-debut%2Cc9261731) identify a specially built Global Rallycross course using the frontstretch and pit area. Its temporary features do not by themselves establish the catalogue Short configuration. [GoPro's official 2012 Charlotte footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0F2POFc7iI) is a further identity lead; the normal public download returned an access error, so its footage has not been checked here.

The [operator's track history](https://www.charlottemotorspeedway.com/fans/track-history/track-history-669197.html) distinguishes the quarter-mile frontstretch oval from its infield courses. The quarter-mile oval alone and simulator configurations cannot replace the unresolved Short route. No nominal-distance match, unlicensed plan pixels, pit-lane detour or invented connector is used to claim a recovery.
